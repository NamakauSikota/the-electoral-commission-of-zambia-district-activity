## Import past reports (CSV)

Use this to load earlier reports (previous elections, by-elections, registration phases). Download the template, paste in one row per report, then upload it. Province must be one of the 10 provinces, activity must match the activity names exactly, and dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Rows missing province, district, activity, dates or summary are skipped.
